W Pompano Beach Hotel & Residences

Seek. Discover. Belong.

Pre-construction, delivery targeted 2029. W Pompano Beach Hotel & Residences brings the W Hotels brand to Pompano Beach: a 24-story oceanfront tower with 77 private residences and 296 condo-hotel suites on 3.75 oceanfront acres with 475 linear feet of private beachfront at 20 North Ocean Boulevard. Suites from $775,000; residences from $3.1M.

Each day at W Pompano Beach Hotel & Residences unfolds in a crescendo of dynamic energy, from the first sign of sunrise to well after sundown — an immersive universe of beach tranquility, natural beauty, vibrant social energy and cultural connection.

Nestled in the heart of Florida’s Gold Coast, located just between Palm Beach and Miami, Pompano Beach is known as one of Florida’s most beautiful shorelines, with untouched expanses of sand and sea. The iconic brand that shaped NYC nightlife for over two decades — evolved — with a playful, refined aesthetic, dynamic arts, culture, and cuisine, and a stunning beachfront setting with panoramic ocean views.

The Residences

Hotel Energy, Private Homes. Seventy-seven private residences: 2 to 4 bedrooms plus den, 2,400 to 3,400 sq ft, with private elevator entry, 9.5-foot ceilings, and views to both the ocean and the Intracoastal from every home.

Penthouses: 3,250 to over 6,200 sq ft, ceilings up to 11 feet, full-width terraces; the top 3 have private rooftop terraces of up to 2,800 sq ft with pools and outdoor kitchens. Interiors by Meyer Davis; kitchens with Italian cabinetry, stone counters, and Wolf and Sub-Zero appliances; every residence has a den, powder room, and walk-in laundry.

The Suites

296 fully furnished condo-hotel suites — studios and 1- and 2-bedroom layouts, 580 to 1,860 sq ft — in the W hotel rental program managed by Marriott International.

The project

20 North Ocean Boulevard, Pompano Beach, FL 33062. A condominium marketed as W Pompano Beach using the W brand of Marriott International, Inc. under license, developed by 20 North Oceanside Owner, LLC. W Pompano Beach is not owned, developed, or sold by Marriott. The Related Group trade names, marks, and logos are also used under license; Related Group is not the developer. One of the first co-located W Hotel and Residences in the Western Hemisphere. Team named on the official site: Related Group (established 1979), BH Group, and KORA Architecture. There is a residential association and a hotel condominium association, each part of a master association.

The Amenities

A 60,000-Square-Foot WET Pool Deck. The beach-club-inspired centerpiece: a 60,000 sq ft WET Pool Deck with a resort pool, private cabanas, full-service bar and grill, pickleball and padel courts, and jacuzzi.

AWAY Spa with custom wellness treatments, cold plunge, jacuzzi, sauna, steam rooms, and hair-and-nail services. FIT fitness center and yoga studio; the Living Room gathering space and social hub with curated programming — wine and food tastings, live music, art exhibitions.

Dining and nightlife: oceanfront restaurant, Living Room lounge, speakeasy, game lounge, F1 racing simulator, multi-sport simulator. Resident-only amenities: private pool and deck with outdoor barbecue area, club lounge, workspace, private fitness center, and a grand room with individual wine lockers; 24-hour attended lobby, valet services, private elevator access.

Services include round-the-clock concierge, private transportation, personal chefs, grocery shopping, dog walking, and plant care. Note: the beach club and hotel amenities require fees in addition to regular assessments, and some amenities and hotel-style services are available only at an additional cost and are not included with purchase.

In the Heart of the Gold Coast

Just between Palm Beach and Miami, on one of Florida’s most beautiful shorelines — a 24-story oceanfront tower beside the pier, with 475 linear feet of private beachfront.
