Pier Sixty-Six Residences

Effortless ownership on the superyacht marina.

Ready for immediate occupancy on the Pier Sixty-Six waterfront: three residence collections — Azul, Indigo, and the Resort Residences — where everyday life runs seamlessly from residence to marina slip, private members-only club, dining, and resort amenities, all on one of Fort Lauderdale’s most storied waterfront addresses. Completed in 2025: 88 residences within a 32-acre resort and marina with a 164-slip superyacht marina, 12 restaurants and bars, and a 13,000 sq ft spa.

The site’s headline theme is “Effortless Ownership”: everyday life is a seamless experience — from your residence to your marina slip, private members-only club, dining, and resort amenities. Developer: Tavistock Development Company.

The Residences

Three collections, each with its own character, all with private terraces and waterfront views.

Azul

Architecture inspired by coral reefs. Distinctive curving terraces overlook the marina, with floor-to-ceiling windows and doors opening to panoramic waterfront views. Soft, natural tones and refined finishes. A signature plunge pool on every expansive terrace — the site states Azul was the first building in Fort Lauderdale to include a private plunge pool for every residence.

  • Residence A: 3 bedrooms, 3.5 bathrooms; interior 2,878 sq ft; terrace 952 sq ft; total 3,830 sq ft.
  • Residence B: 3 bedrooms, 3.5 bathrooms; interior 2,878 sq ft; terrace 980 sq ft; total 3,858 sq ft.
  • Residence C: 3 bedrooms, 3.5 bathrooms; interior 2,718 sq ft; terrace 917 sq ft; total 3,635 sq ft.
  • Residence D: 3 bedrooms, 3.5 bathrooms; interior 2,718 sq ft; terrace 917 sq ft; total 3,635 sq ft.

Indigo

Enjoying prime position on the waterfront, with endless vistas of skyline and waterways. 3–5 bedrooms, effortless open-plan living, and a private plunge pool illuminated in softly glowing indigo on the terrace. Every detail is as carefully curated as the superyacht lifestyle downstairs.

Indigo Penthouse, 10th floor (per the official-site press page): a five-bedroom residence with 5,644 sq ft of interior space, a 2,150 sq ft wraparound terrace, two private plunge pools, private elevator access, and 180-degree views of the marina, Intracoastal Waterway, and Atlantic Ocean; total 7,794 sq ft.

Resort Residences

Ease into effortless living along the peaceful Intracoastal Waterway. Cinematic views of yachts, palm trees, and swimming pools. Loft-inspired 12-foot ceilings, rain showers with integrated lighting, entertainer kitchens, and a private terrace or garden. Two low-rise buildings along the Intracoastal.

All dimensions on the site are stated as approximate and subject to change with actual construction; floor plans and development plans are subject to change.

The Amenities
