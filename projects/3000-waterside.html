3000 Waterside

A collection of waterfront residences in Fort Lauderdale.

Pre-construction, delivery estimated Q2 2028. An exclusive collection of 129 Sky Residences in an 18-story tower directly on the Intracoastal Waterway, with two- to four-bedroom residences from 1,613 to 2,664 sq ft, from $999,999 — plus a waterfront pool deck, an on-site restaurant, and a 19th-floor roof deck with panoramic views.

Step into a world of elevated luxury and effortless living, Fort Lauderdale’s most coveted waterfront residences. Located directly on the serene Intracoastal Waterway, this exclusive community of Sky Residences offers a rare opportunity to experience five-star amenities and spectacular views, all from the comfort of your own home.

Each of the 129 residences is individually tailored to reflect the contours of the building and its unrivaled waterfront location.

The Residences

Sky Residences on the Intracoastal. 129 Sky residences in an 18-story tower, with large balconies and terraces — including penthouses on the top 4 floors. 13 inviting floor plans with intuitive open design, including 5 penthouse designs on the top 4 floors.

Two-, three-, and four-bedroom Sky residences with two, three, or three-and-a-half bathrooms, ranging from 1,613 to 2,664 interior square feet. Many floor plans include a spacious den/media room with floor-to-ceiling impact windows and sliding glass doors. Expansive glass-framed private balconies and terraces with views of the Intracoastal, Atlantic Ocean, and Fort Lauderdale skyline.

Large-format (24" x 48") porcelain tile flooring throughout; spacious laundry rooms and closets with energy-efficient Electrolux washer and dryer. Each residence includes one storage unit and one dedicated parking space, with additional spaces available for purchase; EV charging wiring available as an upgrade. Penthouse units over 1,693 sq ft come with two parking spaces.

Kitchens and bathrooms

Gourmet contemporary European kitchens with custom cabinetry by Poggenpohl and quartz countertops and backsplash; Miele appliances including integrated refrigerator, wall oven, electric cooktop, recirculating hood, dishwasher, microwave, and Edge Star wine cooler. Penthouse units (except units 5 and 6 on levels 15–17) feature a speed oven in place of the microwave.

Sophisticated primary suites with designer-ready walk-in closets and spa-inspired bath retreats with premium fixtures, bathtubs and/or walk-in showers; free-standing soaking tub and hand-held showers in penthouse primary bedrooms (and where applicable); contemporary European vanities and quartz countertops.

Pricing and delivery

Starting at $999,999, as stated on the official homepage; developer materials quoted up to $3,366,320. Groundbreaking scheduled for fall 2026; estimated delivery Q2 2028. Deposit schedule: 20% at signing, 10% at pouring of the 2nd floor, 5% at rooftop, balance at closing.

The project

3000 East Oakland Park Boulevard, Fort Lauderdale, FL 33306. Developed by Claridge Homes — an award-winning international developer founded in 1986, with over 200 completed projects and nearly 20,000 families housed. Architecture by FSMY Architects; landscape architect Natalia Barranco; interior design by Manhas Design and ARCHiflux.

The Amenities

Five-star amenities on the water.

  • Waterfront pool deck with Intracoastal views, resort-style pool and spa, and barbecue courtyard.
  • Waterside Restaurant offering gourmet cuisine, 4,600 sq ft of indoor/outdoor dining, plus valet parking for restaurant guests.
  • State-of-the-art fitness and wellness center with a yoga studio, spa oasis, and treatment room.
  • 19th-floor roof deck: 8,500 sq ft of space, outdoor bar, two barbecues, two fire pits, dog walk, and panoramic views.
  • Luxury lobby: double-height, fully furnished spaces designed by Manhas Design and ARCHiflux, overlooking the Intracoastal Waterway.
  • Private party room with bar, pool views, and warming kitchen; deluxe billiards room on the main level.
  • Guest suites: two available for rent by condo owners for a daily fee.
  • Boat docks: up to 13 docks available for purchase by condo owners (at developer’s discretion), plus 1 dock for restaurant guest pick-up/drop-off. Dockage $500,000, per developer materials.
  • 24/7 concierge services with white-glove service in the lobby; lush landscaping with accent lighting, creating a private oasis.

Coral Ridge, on the Water

3000 Waterside sits in Coral Ridge, directly on the Intracoastal Waterway — minutes from Fort Lauderdale Beach, Las Olas, and Fort Lauderdale International Airport.
