The project

A private waterfront retreat, between ocean and bay

The St. Regis Resort & Residences Bahia Mar defines the standard and creates a legacy like no other. Surrounded by water in every direction, life here unfolds with grace. At its heart, the iconic 200-room St. Regis Hotel anchors a world where a pristine beach, deepwater marina slips, an exclusive full-service Beach Club, elevated culinary experiences, and restorative spa indulgences blend relaxed sophistication with the comforts of home.

Each element has been carefully considered, complete with sweeping terraces and panoramic views, fully serviced amenities, personalized Butler Service, and signature St. Regis rituals. At Bahia Mar, daily life becomes a collection of beautifully lived moments.

A $2 billion redevelopment of the 40-acre Bahia Mar site by Related Group, Tate Capital, and Rok Acquisitions, in the yachting capital of the world — where beaches, Intracoastal waterways, and a marina meet. Groundbreaking 2027, top-off 2028, estimated completion Q4 2029.

The residences

A life of distinction

The St. Regis Bahia Mar offers an exquisite lifestyle within expansive private homes in a dedicated residential tower and fully serviced, turnkey resort residences. Each is designed for seamless connection to the water, with interiors by Tara Bernerd & Partners that feature soaring spaces and panoramic ocean-to-bay views, and access to the full suite of St. Regis amenities.

Interiors by Tara Bernerd & Partners: natural materials, layered finishes, and a subdued coastal palette. Kitchens feature an expansive island, natural stone countertops, fully integrated Sub-Zero and Wolf appliances including wine storage and a speed oven, and custom cabinetry.

Private Residences Tower

A 23-story boutique tower with 80 residences, four per floor. Three-bedroom-plus-den and four-bedroom-plus-den layouts, 2,600 to 3,550 sq ft, from about $5M. HOA about $2.00 per sq ft. Two penthouses with wraparound terraces and four parking spaces.

Private elevators and foyers, 10-ft ceilings, Wolf and Sub-Zero appliances, Kallista and Dornbracht fixtures, Italian marble bathrooms, freestanding tubs, and walk-in closets. Two assigned parking spaces per residence.

  • Dedicated porte-cochère and private lobby
  • 24-hour concierge
  • Signature St. Regis Butler Service
  • Keyed elevators opening directly into each residence
  • Sweeping terraces and panoramic views

Tower amenities — 37,000 sq ft

24/7 concierge and attended lobby, private beach club (1,800 linear ft), pool, hot tubs, luxury spa, and fitness center. Pickleball, bicycles, and a children/teen room. Library, media room, billiards, and sports simulator.

Bahia Club — owners’ private park, exclusively for tower residences

Lap pool, tennis courts, padel, wellness green, outdoor fitness pavilion, fire pits, shaded lounge areas, and a summer kitchen.

Residences at the Resort

106 furnished units starting on the 10th floor. Two- to three-bedroom residences plus four-bedroom multi-level penthouses, with private elevators, terraces, penthouse pools, and summer kitchens, and the same luxury finishes and appliances as the tower. Valet parking only. From about $3M. HOA about $1.80 per sq ft.

Resort residences can be rented nightly, weekly, or long-term, with access to hotel services, events, and amenities, plus a dedicated resident app.

Resort amenities — 65,000 sq ft

Private lobby, lounge, and elevators. Two resort-style pools, fitness center, and 5,000 sq ft spa. Restaurants, poolside bar and grill, and a cognac and tea room. Beach club access, children’s club, meeting rooms, and ballrooms. Butler service, house cars, and two acres of green space.

The amenities

Designed for living well

At The St. Regis Resort & Residences Bahia Mar, every part of the day is elevated, with sunrise swims and sunset cocktails seamlessly interwoven with wellness experiences and beachside relaxation.

Water is central to the experiences, with a world-class marina, pristine beach, and resort-style pools offering endless ways to feel refreshed and relaxed.

Beach Club

An exclusive, full-service St. Regis Beach Club, reached by a private footbridge to the beach — with personalized service including food and beverage, towels, umbrellas, and water toys.

Bahia Mar Marina

A landmark marina at the heart of Bahia Mar: 250 deepwater slips welcoming vessels up to 350 feet, with direct ocean access.

Bahia Mar Yacht Club

Complimentary Bahia Mar Yacht Club membership extended to residents, with access to a fleet of yachts and custom sailing experiences including snorkeling, jet-skiing, paddleboarding, and sport fishing.

Pools & spa

Resort-style pools — the East Pool and the West Pool — with expansive pool decks, light-filled fitness centers with water views, and The Residences Spa with private treatment rooms, sauna, steam rooms, and a secluded garden with hot and cold plunge pools.

Bahia Mar Club

An exclusive residents-only club on one acre of landscaped grounds, shaped around wellness and longevity — movement, restoration, and connection. Active mornings, leisurely afternoons by the pool, and evenings gathering with family and friends. Private entry and a residents’ pool.

Dining & services

Signature fine dining and a variety of culinary experiences, with exclusive private dining. Personalized Butler Service, 24-hour concierge, and signature St. Regis rituals.

Good to know: some amenities and hotel-style services are available only at an additional cost and are not included with purchase. The beach club may require fees in addition to regular assessments. A marina slip lease requires payment in addition to assessments and is first come, first served. The marina is owned and operated by a third party.

Location

A world within reach

At the meeting point of city and sea, Bahia Mar connects you to the best of Fort Lauderdale. From Las Olas Boulevard to the Brightline, private yacht slips to international and executive airports, this one-of-a-kind coastal setting — enhanced by world-class dining, luxury shopping, and vibrant cultural and art offerings — delivers beauty, access, and ease.
